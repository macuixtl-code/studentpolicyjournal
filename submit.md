# Submit Your Work

We are here to support you. Our mission is to encourage a diverse range of perspectives, including from individuals who have not written for publication before. Our editors will strive to provide all the resources necessary for writers to craft, edit, and publish their pieces.

## What We Accept

- Policy Memos
- Research Articles
- Policy Briefs
- Essays
- Policy Commentary

## Submission Requirements
This journal seeks submissions of policy-oriented student work including journal-style academic analysis, think tank-style policy briefs and memos, and research-driven opinion work on policy issues. That includes but is not limited to:

Long Form
- Suggested length: <10,000 words
- Include citations for outside sources
  
Short Form
- Suggested Length: <1000 words
- Topics: Well-researched opinion pieces on hot-button topics / Commentary highlighting important policy issues that don’t make the headlines

## Submission Guidelines
Submissions should follow the guidelines below and be submitted via our Google Form. We are open to submissions from all USC students.
- Submissions should be evidence-based, policy-focused, and analytically oriented. They should analyze an issue in public policy, including identifying its causes and possible solutions, rather than merely describing an issue.
- Submissions may be reworked versions of assignments, such as policy memos and term papers. If your submission was written for class, we ask that you reformat it as needed to comply with these guidelines.
- All submissions should contain citations in Chicago Style (author-date). Listed citations should contain URLs if materials were accessed online.
- Submissions should be uploaded in .docx format
- Submissions should be original, not generated or substantially drafted by AI. If AI was used to help research or edit the piece, we simply ask that you add a brief acknowledgement of that use at the end of your references section. 
- Submissions that interpret or process data must share the raw data, processing methods, and/or replication files

*Please note, adherence to these guidelines does not guarantee publication of your piece.*
