# Submit Your Work

We welcome original submissions from students and emerging writers.

## What We Accept

- Research articles
- Policy briefs
- Essays
- Commentary

## Submission Requirements

- Suggested length: 1,000–3,000 words
- Include citations for outside sources
- Include a short author biography
- Submit work in Word or Google Docs format

## How to Submit

Email your submission to: your-email@example.com
